How to Calculate CGPA

1. Enter the name of each subject.

2. Enter its credit value and grade point (0 to 10).

3. Add more subjects as needed.

4. Click Calculate CGPA to see your result.

CGPA Formula

CGPA = Sum of (Credits × Grade Points) ÷ Total Credits

This calculator uses a credit-weighted 10-point scale. Your university may have its own grading and CGPA conversion rules. Enter grade points according to your institution's grading system.
