About Our Image Compressor

This free tool helps reduce image file sizes for websites, assignments and everyday use. Choose an image, adjust its quality and download the result.

Images are processed locally in your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers. The result is saved as JPEG, so transparency and some image metadata are not preserved.

Some images may become larger after conversion, especially small or highly optimized files. Always compare the output size before downloading.
