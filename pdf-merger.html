Free Online PDF Merger

Combine multiple PDF documents into one file. Fast, free and private.

How to Merge PDF Files

1. Select two or more PDF documents.

2. Use the arrow buttons to arrange the files in your preferred order.

3. Click Merge PDF Files to create a combined document.

4. Download your merged PDF automatically.

Private PDF Processing

Your PDF files are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers. The JavaScript library is loaded from a third-party CDN.

Password-protected or damaged PDFs may not be supported. Very large files may require significant device memory.
