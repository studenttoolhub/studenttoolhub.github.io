Free PDF Splitter

Extract specific pages from a PDF and create a new PDF file. Free, simple and secure.

Select your PDF file

Use commas to separate pages or ranges. Example: 1-3, 5, 8-10.

Privacy: Your PDF is processed locally in your browser. It is not uploaded to our server.

How to split a PDF?

  1. Choose a PDF file from your device.
  2. Enter the pages or page ranges you need.
  3. Click Split PDF.
  4. Download your new PDF.
