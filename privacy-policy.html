Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Welcome to Student Tool Hub. We respect your privacy and aim to explain clearly how our website and free online tools handle information.

1. Information You Provide

Student Tool Hub currently provides tools that can be used without creating an account. You may enter text, grades, credits, or select files when using our tools.

2. File and Text Processing

Our current Word Counter, Image Compressor, PDF Merger, and CGPA Calculator are designed to process the information you provide within your web browser. We do not operate a backend server that receives the files or text entered into these tools.

However, the website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and some tools load JavaScript libraries from third-party content delivery networks. These services may receive technical information associated with requests made by your browser.

3. Cookies and Analytics

We do not currently include our own advertising or analytics tracking scripts in these tools. Third-party services involved in hosting or delivering website resources may handle technical information according to their own privacy policies.

If we introduce analytics, advertising, or additional cookie-based services in the future, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Some features may use external JavaScript libraries, including pdf-lib, delivered through a CDN.

Please review the privacy practices of relevant third-party providers for more information about their handling of technical data.

5. Data Security

Browser-based processing helps limit the need to transmit your document contents to our servers. However, no website or internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Avoid entering highly sensitive personal information unless you are comfortable with the tool and your device's security.

6. Children's Privacy

Our tools are intended for general educational and productivity use. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through account registration or submission forms.

7. Advertising

Student Tool Hub may display advertisements in the future. If advertising services are added, we will update this policy to explain relevant data practices, including any applicable cookies and consent options.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, tools, or third-party services change. The latest version will be available on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use our Contact page once it becomes available.
